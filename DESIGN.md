# Velo design direction

## Product read

Velo gives people a public record of the calls they actually stand behind. The landing page is for a curious visitor who needs a clear consumer idea. The app is for someone ready to inspect live windows and make a Call.

## Visual thesis

An editorial record book with a live green signal. The landing page uses an off-white field, an ink headline, and one conceptual Call card to make the product memorable without inventing market activity. The app keeps the same materials but becomes a calm instrument for choosing a live window.

## System

- Surface: warm paper, white record surfaces, and deep forest ink.
- Text: dark green for primary content, moss gray for context.
- Accent: vivid green for live state, primary action, and selected controls.
- Type: system sans for readable product copy, monospaced metadata for market facts, Georgia italic for the editorial emphasis.
- Shape: pill buttons for actions, thin ruled containers for records, restrained borders, no glass effects.
- Rhythm: generous whitespace on the landing page, compact two-column market rows in the app, single-column flow on mobile.

## Interaction

The primary path is Open Velo, which moves from the consumer landing page to `/app`. The app loads only verified live windows, opens a focused Call composer, and gives clear feedback while a wallet transaction and public record save are in progress. Public Calls keep proof behind a secondary View proof action.

## Truth constraints

The landing page contains no wallet connection, live market data, fake profiles, fake results, or technical implementation language. The app and public routes render only live data and records returned by the existing DreamDEX and Velo persistence layers.
